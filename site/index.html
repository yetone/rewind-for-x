See the posts your timeline just replaced

X refreshes Home and the post you were halfway through is gone. Rewind for X keeps every post you've seen, right in your browser.

Add to Chrome GitHub

Kept as you scroll

Once half a post has been on screen, its author, text, images, quoted post and link are saved. Ads are skipped.

One key away

Open the drawer from the button above X's own, the toolbar icon or Alt Shift R. Newest first, grouped by when you saw them.

Opens in a new tab

Click a post to read it on X without refreshing Home and losing your place again.

Search everything

Find a post by author, @handle, any word in it, or the text inside its images.

Filter by kind

Show only posts with images, videos, quotes or reposts.

Matches X

Follows X's light, dim and dark themes, on desktop and narrow windows alike.

Your history stays on your device

Posts are stored in your browser's local extension storage. No accounts, no analytics, nothing uploaded.

The newest 3,000 posts are kept. Clear them all with two clicks.

Read the privacy policy

Install

  1. Download the extension and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder.
  4. Open or refresh x.com/home.
